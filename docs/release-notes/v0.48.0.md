Win顺 0.48.0：剪贴板历史能备份成一个 ZIP、换电脑后还原；“文件”的类型标签上显示每种类型找到多少个；增强文字识别可以加装读日文的较大模型，也能用自己下载的模型；拖文件时的收藏圆盘在文件夹上停一下就展开子文件夹（#463）；录屏控制条按住空白处就能拖。全部图标改为按容器排版，选项标签不再是胶囊形。

## 新功能 · What's new

- 💾 **剪贴板历史备份与还原**：**设置 › 剪贴板 › 保留 › 备份与还原** 点“备份…”，把全部历史、分组、备注、单条快捷键和图片存成一个 ZIP。换电脑或重装系统后在同一处点“还原…”选它，确认后 Win顺 重启一次，历史就回来了。还原是整体替换现在的历史，会先问一句；不是剪贴板备份的文件、损坏的文件都会被拒绝，原来的历史不动。

  **Back up and restore the clipboard history**: 备份… under **Settings › Clipboard › Keeping › Back up and restore** saves the whole history, its groups, notes, entry shortcuts and pictures into one ZIP. On a new PC or after reinstalling Windows, 还原… in the same place takes that file; after you confirm, WinShun restarts once and the history is back. A restore replaces the present history whole, so it asks first; a file that is not a clipboard backup, or a damaged one, is turned away with nothing changed.

- 🔢 **类型标签上显示数量**：在“文件”里输入后，每个类型的名字右边有个小数字，是当前输入在这个类型里找到多少个；“不限”是总数。选中某个类型时其他类型的数字照样显示，一眼看出该点哪个。超过 9999 写成“1.2万”。不想要可在 **设置 › 结果列表 › 文件类型** 关掉“类型标签显示数量”（默认开）。

  **Counts on the type chips**: with something typed in Files, each type's chip carries a small number after its name: how many matches are of that type (不限: all of them). The other chips keep their numbers while one is chosen, so you see which to pick. Above 9999 a number is shortened (12k). Switch it off under **Settings › Result list › File types** (on by default).

- 🈶 **增强文字识别能读日文，也能用自己的模型**：增强识别自带的小模型没有假名，日文读出来是乱的。**设置 › 剪贴板 › 记录 › 增强文字识别** 现在可以加装较大的识别模型（约 21 MB，按需下载，可卸载），日文、更多汉字都能读；也可以点“用自己下载的模型…”选任意 PP-OCR 识别模型（ONNX），不是识别模型的文件会被拒绝并说明原因。换模型后，剪贴板里的图片会重新读一遍。

  **Enhanced OCR reads Japanese, and takes a model of your own**: the small bundled reading model has no kana, so Japanese came out as noise. **Settings › Clipboard › Recording › Enhanced OCR** can now add a larger reading model (about 21 MB, downloaded on request, removable) that reads Japanese and many more Han characters, or take any PP-OCR recognition model (ONNX) you downloaded through 用自己下载的模型…; a file that is no reading model is refused with the reason. After a change of model the clipboard's pictures are read again.

- 🗂️ **收藏圆盘里展开子文件夹**（#463）：拖着文件按 `` ` `` 弹出圆盘后，在文件夹上停一下，旁边展开它的子文件夹，和文件夹菜单一样一列一列往里走；在哪一行松开就放进哪个文件夹。往展开的那一列移动时路过别的文件夹不会切换，停回中间就全部收起。

  **Subfolders in the drop pie** (#463): with the pie up while dragging files (`` ` ``), rest on a folder and its subfolders open in a column beside it, column after column as in the folder menu; let go on a row to drop there. Heading for an open column does not switch to the folders passed on the way, and resting in the middle closes them all.

- 🎥 **录屏控制条按住空白处就能拖，保存文件夹进了设置**：控制条不再只能按左端手柄拖，边距、文字、按钮之间的空隙都行（按钮上按住拖不会移动它）。**截图设置**里多了“录屏保存文件夹”，和录屏选项里“保存到”是同一个设置（截图与录屏 1.1.38）。

  **The recording bar moves from any empty place; its folder is in the settings**: drag the bar by its margins, words or the gaps between buttons, not only the grip (a press on a button stays a press). The screenshot settings have a Recording folder row, the same setting as 保存到 in the recording options (Screenshot and recording 1.1.38).

## 改进与修复 · Improvements and fixes

- **图标按容器排版，选项标签不再是胶囊形**：所有图标改成放在固定的偶数方格里，周围的文字和边缘只和方格保持间距，图标和文字之间、图标和选项边缘之间的距离因此一致。类型标签、内容的文件夹和种类、剪贴板分组、筛选项改成小圆角；带图标的选项，图标到左边和到上下的距离相等。

  **Icons laid out by their container; chips are no longer capsules**: every icon now stands in a fixed even square and what is beside it keeps its distance from that square, so gaps between icons, words and edges are consistent. The type chips, Content's folders and kinds, the clipboard's groups and the filter chips take a small corner radius, and a chip's icon is as far from its left edge as from its top and bottom.

- **插件的“使用说明”和“更新内容”**：拖滚动条后不再一片空白，滚动时文字不再抖，标题、列表、代码、引用和表格按阅读排版。

  **A plugin's 使用说明 and 更新内容**: no blank page after a drag of the scroll bar, no shaking text while scrolling, and headings, lists, code, quotes and tables are laid out for reading.

- **插件开发**：`wsbench` 的 `--plugin`、`--launcher-sim`、`--mcp-host` 可加 `--app-dir <目录>`，让声明 `"qt": "host"` 的插件用已安装 Win顺 的 Qt 运行（#462）。

  **Plugin development**: `wsbench --plugin`, `--launcher-sim` and `--mcp-host` take `--app-dir DIR`, so a plugin declaring `"qt": "host"` runs with an installed WinShun's Qt (#462).
