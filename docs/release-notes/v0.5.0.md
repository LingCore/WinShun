## 新功能 · What's new

- 📄 **搜索文档里的文字**：文件内容搜索现在也能搜 Word、Excel、PowerPoint（新旧格式都行）、PDF 和 WPS 文件，结果写明在第几页、第几张幻灯片，或哪个工作表的第几行。文档在一个权限受限的独立进程里读取，读出的文字存在本机，下次不用再读。搜一行代码也更准，像 `index->readLock();` 这样带标点的也能直接搜。
  **Search the text in documents**: content search now covers Word, Excel and PowerPoint files (old and new formats), PDF and WPS files, and says the page, the slide, or the sheet and row. Documents are read by a separate, locked-down process, and the text is kept on your PC so they are not read again. Lines of code are found more precisely too, punctuation and all, like `index->readLock();`.
- 🌐 **网页搜索关键词**：给常用网站设一个关键词，输入关键词回车就打开它；地址里带 `%s` 的还能直接搜，比如 `gh WinShun` 在 GitHub 上搜。在设置 → 网页搜索里添加和修改。
  **Keywords for web searches**: give a site a keyword; type it and press Enter to open the site, or, when its address has `%s`, search it: `gh WinShun` searches GitHub. Add and change them in Settings → Web search.
- 📋 **剪贴板在打字的地方弹出**：剪贴板现在是一个独立的小窗口，出现在正在输入的那一行下面。在 Win顺 自己的输入框里打开，它就展开在输入框下面，选中的内容直接粘贴进去。点一条就粘贴，`Alt+P` 或点眼睛按钮在旁边打开预览。“最多保留”可选 50 到 300 条，默认 200 条（内存 16 GB 及以下默认 100 条）；以前设成 500 条以上的按 300 条算，超出的旧记录会被删除，固定和分组里的不受影响。
  **A clipboard that opens where you type**: the clipboard is now a small window of its own, under the line you are typing in. Opened from one of WinShun's own text boxes, it drops down under that box and pastes into it. Click an entry to paste it; `Alt+P` or the eye button opens the preview beside the list. “Keep at most” goes from 50 to 300 entries, 200 by default (100 with 16 GB of memory or less); a limit of 500 or more set before becomes 300 and older entries beyond it are deleted, while pinned and grouped ones stay.
- 🎮 **玩游戏时双击 Ctrl 不再打扰**：很多游戏用 Ctrl 蹲下。现在前台游戏独占全屏，或者藏起鼠标转视角时，双击 Ctrl 不会弹出搜索框；还可以选择任何程序全屏时都不响应，或者把某些程序加进排除列表。另设的快捷键照常可用。双击 Ctrl 也更准了：触摸板缩放、拖文件时按 Ctrl 不会误触发，间隔跟随系统的双击速度。
  **Double Ctrl leaves your games alone**: many games crouch on Ctrl. Double Ctrl no longer opens the search box over a game in exclusive full screen or one that has hidden the mouse pointer to turn the view; optionally not over anything full screen either, or never over programs you list. Your other shortcut still works. Double Ctrl is also more reliable: touchpad zooming and pressing Ctrl while dragging files no longer set it off, and the gap between the taps follows your double-click speed.
- 📂 **对话框旁的搜索框会找地方**：“打开”“另存为”对话框下面放不下时，搜索框放到对话框左边或右边，还放不下就把对话框调小一点；也可以在设置里固定放在下方、左边或右边。
  **The file-dialog search bar finds room**: when there is no room under an Open or Save dialog, the search bar goes on its left or right, and where nothing fits the dialog is made a little smaller; you can also fix it below, left or right in the settings.
- 💾 **索引可以放到别的盘**：设置 → 高级 → 索引位置，可以把索引移到别的文件夹，移动时照常搜索。索引文件缩小到原来的三分之一，保存和加载更快。
  **The index can live on another disk**: Settings → Advanced → Index location moves the index to another folder while you keep searching. The index file is a third of its old size, and saves and loads faster.
- ✨ **其他**：搜“计算器”和“Calculator”都能找到 Windows 自带的应用；开始菜单开着时双击 Ctrl，键盘也会到搜索框；窗口圆角的边缘更平滑；设置页、对话框搜索框和剪贴板滚动停下时文字不再跳动；后台更省电。
  **Also**: Windows' own apps are found by their Chinese and English names (“计算器” and “Calculator”); double Ctrl with the Start menu open now gets the keyboard; rounded window corners have smoother edges; text in the settings, the file-dialog search bar and the clipboard no longer jumps as scrolling stops; and less work in the background.
