## 新功能 · What's new

- 🎬 **录屏更清楚，最高 60 帧，可加鼠标拖尾**：画质分小体积、标准、高清、超清四档。超清把不超过 1920×1080 的区域按两倍大小录下来，彩色文字和细线不再发糊，录屏条上会写出实际保存的尺寸。MP4 的帧率单独选 15、30 或 60。录屏选项排成两列，占的地方更少。“鼠标”一行在“点击高亮”后面多了“鼠标拖尾”：一条细线跟着指针走，停下就收回去。没能按选的方式录（没录成两倍大小，或帧率不够）时，面板会说明原因和该改什么。录到一半不想要了，点垃圾桶按钮“放弃本次录制（不保存）”，MP4 和 GIF 都行。

  **Clearer recordings, up to 60 frames a second, with a cursor trail**: four qualities, Smaller file, Standard, High and Ultra. Ultra records a region up to 1920×1080 at twice its size, so coloured text and thin lines stay sharp, and the bar shows the size that is saved. MP4's frame rate is a choice of its own: 15, 30 or 60. The options take two columns and less room. The 鼠标 row has 鼠标拖尾 after 点击高亮: a thin line follows the pointer and draws back when it stops. When a recording could not be saved as chosen (not at twice the size, or fewer frames a second), the panel says why and what to change. A recording you no longer want can be thrown away midway with the bin button, MP4 and GIF alike.

- 📌 **贴图分组、截完直接贴、找回关掉的贴图、存回原文件**：贴图可以分组（右键 › 分组，托盘菜单 › 贴图分组，或者 `cap 分组 名字` 新建），一键切换显示哪组，也能设“切换贴图分组”的快捷键（#451）。新的“截图并直接贴图”快捷键：拖选区域或单击窗口，松开就贴在原处，不出工具条（#450）。剪贴板没变时连按贴图快捷键，会按顺序把之前截过或关掉的图贴回来，标注还在；记几张、按什么顺序在 截图设置 › 贴图 里设（#446）。从文件打开的图片编辑后，`Ctrl+S` 存回原文件，“另存为”和“保存到截图文件夹”还在下拉菜单里（#427）。

  **Pin groups, pin as you capture, closed pins back, save to the original file**: pins can be grouped (right click › 分组, the tray menu's 贴图分组, or `cap 分组 name` to make one), one group shown at a time, with a 切换贴图分组 shortcut if you like (#451). A new 截图并直接贴图 shortcut: drag a region or click a window and it is pinned in place on release, with no toolbar (#450). While the clipboard has not changed, pressing the pin key again brings back earlier screenshots and closed pins in turn, marks included; how many are kept and in which order is under Screenshot settings › 贴图 (#446). A picture opened from a file saves back to it with `Ctrl+S`; Save As and Save to Screenshots stay in the drop-down (#427).

- 🔦 **聚光灯**：截图和编辑里的新工具（快捷键 `G`），框出一块或几块（矩形或椭圆，按住 `Shift` 画正方形或圆），其余部分压暗；压暗程度可选 40%、60%（默认）、80%、100%（100% 就是整片遮住），滚轮或 `[` `]` 微调，颜色可选或用取色器。

  **Spotlight**: a new tool in the screenshot and the editor (`G`): mark one or more areas (rectangles or ellipses, `Shift` for squares and circles) and the rest dims by 40%, 60% (the default), 80% or 100% (covering it fully), fine-tuned with the wheel or `[` `]`, in a colour of your choice or picked from the screen.

- 📜 **长截图自己往下滚**：开始后连续滚动，速度分慢速、标准、快速，点一下就换；`Enter` 完成，`Esc` 取消。拼接更稳，动画和细微的抖动不再让它以为“跟丢了”；新的长图预览写着宽高，可以直接贴图、保存、复制。

  **Long screenshots scroll by themselves**: once started, the page scrolls on its own at 慢速, 标准 or 快速 (a click switches); `Enter` finishes, `Esc` cancels. Stitching holds up better: animations and one-pixel jitter no longer make it think it lost its place. The new preview shows the width and height and can pin, save or copy straight away.

- 🔎 **模糊匹配（FZF）**：设置 › 结果列表 › 显示与预览 › 附加搜索源与匹配模式 里三选一。默认“无结果时”：平常的搜索完全不变，什么都找不到时才按 FZF 跳字找（至少 3 个字），列表顶上会写明。“始终开启”时模糊结果排在正常结果后面；“关闭”就不自动用。任何时候都能在词前加 `fz:` 按需用，比如 `fz:rpt` 找 `Report.pdf`（#422）。

  **Fuzzy matching (FZF)**: one of three in Settings › Result list › 显示与预览 › 附加搜索源与匹配模式. The default, 无结果时, leaves the usual search as it is and only falls back to FZF subsequence matching (three characters or more) when nothing is found, saying so at the top. 始终开启 adds fuzzy matches after the usual ones; 关闭 never does it by itself. `fz:` in front of a word asks for it any time: `fz:rpt` finds `Report.pdf` (#422).

- ⏻ **在搜索框里关机、重启、睡眠、锁屏**：输入“关机”“重启”“睡眠”“休眠”“锁屏”“注销”（英文也行）就有对应的一行。除了锁屏，都要在 5 秒内再按一次 `Enter` 或再点一次才执行，不会误按；电脑不支持睡眠或休眠时不列出（#428）。

  **Shut down, restart, sleep or lock from the search box**: type 关机, 重启, 睡眠, 休眠, 锁屏 or 注销 (or the English words) for the matching row. Except for locking, each needs a second `Enter` or click within 5 seconds, so nothing happens by accident; sleep and hibernate are listed only where the PC supports them (#428).

- 🗑️ **快捷菜单里“移到回收站”**：搜索结果的快捷菜单可以把选中的文件和文件夹移到回收站，多选时不在同一个文件夹里的也行；它标成红色。清除搜索历史的按钮换成了扫帚，不再像删文件（#434）。

  **Move to Recycle Bin in the quick menu**: a result's quick menu moves the chosen files and folders to the Recycle Bin, several from different folders at once, in red. The clear-history button is a broom now, so it no longer looks like deleting files (#434).

- 🔠 **`Ctrl++` / `Ctrl+-` / `Ctrl+0` 调整界面大小**：搜索框、搜索面板、设置窗口、剪贴板和对话框旁的搜索框里都能用，屏幕上会提示现在是哪一档；设置窗口的字和控件跟着一起变大变小。

  **`Ctrl++` / `Ctrl+-` / `Ctrl+0` for the interface size**: in the search box, the search panel, Settings, the clipboard and the box beside Open and Save dialogs, with a note saying which size you are on; the settings window's text and controls follow it.

- 📋 **剪贴板默认粘贴为纯文本，微信里复制的图片能贴到屏幕**：设置 › 剪贴板 里的“默认粘贴为纯文本”（默认关）打开后，单击、`Enter`、`Alt+1–9` 粘贴的文字都不带格式，`Shift+Enter`、`Alt+Shift+数字` 或菜单里的“保留格式粘贴”保留格式。微信、QQ、资源管理器复制的图片是以文件形式进剪贴板的，现在也能“贴到屏幕”（`F3`）和“编辑”（`Ctrl+E`），几张一起贴最多 10 张。

  **Paste words as plain text by default; pin pictures copied in WeChat**: with 默认粘贴为纯文本 on in Settings › Clipboard (off by default), a click, `Enter` and `Alt+1–9` paste words without their formatting, while `Shift+Enter`, `Alt+Shift+number` or the menu's 保留格式粘贴 keep it. Pictures copied in WeChat, QQ or File Explorer arrive as files; they can now be pinned to the screen (`F3`) and edited (`Ctrl+E`), up to ten pinned together.

- 🖱️ **鼠标手势可以用中键、侧键和组合键**：在手势设置里选“触发手势的鼠标按键”，右键、中键、侧键可以同时开；还能做“按住一个键、再点另一个键”的组合手势（左键只当组合的第二个键，平常的点击拖动不受影响）（鼠标手势 2.1.3，#436）。

  **Gestures with the middle and side buttons, and button chords**: choose 触发手势的鼠标按键 in the gesture settings, right, middle and side buttons together if you like, and make chords such as "hold one button, click another" (the left button only ever as the second, so ordinary clicks and drags are untouched) (Mouse gestures 2.1.3, #436).

- 📦 **截图随 Win顺 附带，第一次设置不用联网**：安装包和便携包里带着截图与录屏组件，欢迎引导里打开截图时直接从本机装好，之后照常从插件库更新。引导默认打开剪贴板历史和截图，截图快捷键优先用 `F1`（被占用时依次用 `Ctrl+Alt+A`、`Ctrl+Alt+S`、`Ctrl+Alt+X`）。

  **The screenshot comes with WinShun; first setup needs no network**: the setup and the portable zip carry the screenshot and recording component, so turning it on in the welcome guide installs it from this PC; it updates from the plugin library afterwards. The guide starts with the clipboard history and the screenshot on, the screenshot on `F1` (or `Ctrl+Alt+A`, `Ctrl+Alt+S`, `Ctrl+Alt+X` when that is taken).

- 💡 **自己选要哪些提示**：对话框旁的搜索框不打字时的建议来源可以逐个开关：打开着的文件夹、剪贴板里的路径、收藏、最近的文件夹（#438）。“悬停提示”分成界面与按钮、结果行操作、文件夹菜单路径三类，分别开关（#440）。

  **Choose your hints**: the box beside Open and Save dialogs lets you pick what it suggests before you type: open folders, a path on the clipboard, favorites, recent folders (#438). 悬停提示 splits into 界面与按钮 (interface and buttons), 结果行操作 (row actions) and 文件夹菜单路径 (folder menu paths), each with its own switch (#440).

## 改进与修复 · Improvements and fixes

- **安装完点“完成”才出现**：以前装完时，欢迎引导或搜索框在安装程序的最后一页还开着时就跳出来，再点“完成”又弹一次。现在 Win顺 在后台先准备好，点“完成”后，勾着“运行 Win顺”才出现（第一次安装是欢迎引导，升级是搜索框），没勾就什么都不弹。

  **Nothing shows until Setup's Finish**: the welcome guide or the search box used to pop up while Setup's last page was still open, and Finish brought it up again. WinShun now gets ready in the background and appears only after Finish, when 运行 Win顺 is ticked (the welcome guide on a first install, the search box on an upgrade); unticked, nothing shows.

- **设置页滚轮触边翻页能关掉**：设置 › 外观 里的“设置页滚轮触边翻页”有关闭、轻快、标准、稳重四档。选“关闭”，滚到页面顶部或底部就停在本页，不会切到别的标签；“标准”要停一下再滚约 3 格才切，快速连滚不会误切（#445、#441）。

  **The settings page switch by wheel can be turned off**: 设置页滚轮触边翻页 in Settings › Appearance is 关闭, 轻快, 标准 or 稳重. 关闭 stops at the top or bottom of a page and never switches tabs; 标准 waits for a pause and about three more notches, so a quick flick does not switch (#445, #441).

- **插件库里直接管已装的插件**：已安装插件的卡片标着状态（停用时写“已停用”），带“设置”按钮；点卡片就在插件库里打开它的设置，“返回插件库”回去。

  **Installed plugins managed in the plugin library**: an installed plugin's tile shows its state (已停用 when off) and a 设置 button; clicking it opens its settings right in the library, with 返回插件库 to go back.

- **“内容”里也能按类型筛**：在“内容”里选 图片 或 文档 不再跳到“文件”，只读这几类文件，图片文字搜索的结果也跟着筛（#447）。插件打开图片的那一行，大图和缩略图显示的是图片本身（#448）。剪贴板悬停预览里选中的一段文字，`Ctrl+C` 复制、`Enter` 粘贴（#439）。

  **Kinds of file in 内容 too**: choosing 图片 or 文档 in 内容 no longer jumps to 文件; only those kinds are read, Image text search's rows included (#447). A plugin row that opens a picture shows that picture in 大图 and as its thumbnail (#448). Words selected in the clipboard's hover preview copy with `Ctrl+C` and paste with `Enter` (#439).

- **粘贴到 Photoshop 是选中的那张**：Photoshop、Illustrator 只在切到前台时读剪贴板，以前总粘上一次的图；现在先让它重新读一次再粘贴（#432）。

  **Photoshop pastes the picture you chose**: Photoshop and Illustrator read the clipboard only as they come to the front and kept pasting the previous picture; they now read it again before the paste (#432).

- **Windows 10 上插件又能读剪贴板了**：局域网传输、剪贴板同步在 Windows 10 上读不到别的程序复制的文件和文字，已修好；局域网传输读不到时会在日志里写明原因（#435）。

  **Plugins read the clipboard on Windows 10 again**: LAN transfer and clipboard sync could not read files or words other programs copied on Windows 10; fixed, and LAN transfer logs it when it cannot (#435).

- **翻译密钥不对时会直说**：翻译服务返回 401、403 时显示“翻译服务的密钥不对或没有权限”，按 `Enter` 打开它的设置；截图的文字识别窗口和鼠标手势的划词翻译也这样提示（翻译 2.2.5，#444）。

  **A refused translation key is named as such**: on 401 or 403 the translation says the key is wrong or lacks permission, and `Enter` opens its settings; the screenshot's text window and the gestures' selection translation say so too (Translate 2.2.5, #444).

- **识别文字窗口**：“合并换行”把段落里被折断的行接起来，再点一下还原；复制后显示“已复制”，`Ctrl+Enter` 复制并关闭。序号工具的样式面板更紧凑，起始值用两侧的加减调整。截图工具栏的图标统一成 Phosphor。

  **The recognized-text window**: 合并换行 joins lines broken inside paragraphs and undoes it on a second click; a copy says 已复制, and `Ctrl+Enter` copies and closes. The number tool's style panel is more compact, with − and + either side of the starting value. The screenshot toolbar's icons are all Phosphor now.

- **贴图上的提示挪到图片外面**：翻译中、缩放比例、选文字之类的小提示浮在贴图上方或下方，不再挡住小贴图的内容。演示模式下按 `Esc` 或右键退回时，手里的画笔还在。

  **Pin notes float outside the picture**: hints such as translating, zoom and picking text sit above or below the pin instead of covering a small one. In presenter mode, `Esc` or a right click to step back keeps the pen in hand.

- **资源管理器的“贴到屏幕”支持 WebP、HEIF、AVIF**：以前这些格式右键没有这一项或报错；不想要这一项可以在 设置 › 系统整合 › 文件管理器 里关掉。

  **File Explorer's 贴到屏幕 works on WebP, HEIF and AVIF**: those formats used to lack the entry or fail; it can be turned off under Settings › Integration › File managers.

- **Tessoa 接管的打开、保存窗口**：Tessoa 自己画的那个窗口没有接口可以跳转，Win顺 在旁边只提供复制路径，并说明原因和怎么改用系统窗口；不显示搜索框时只提示一次。XYplorer 的路径读取和 WPS 的自动跳转更稳。

  **Open and Save windows taken over by Tessoa**: Tessoa's own window offers no way to go to a folder, so WinShun's bar beside it only copies paths and says why and how to use Windows' window instead; with the bar off, a notification says it once. XYplorer's path reading and WPS's auto-jump are more reliable.

- **更快、更稳**：空闲一阵后打开搜索框立即显示，不等刷新；窗口被隐藏时不再偶尔卡顿 1 秒才出新结果。开机后第一次打开切换英文输入更可靠。开着透明效果时，搜索面板展开后不再透出旧式的窗口边框；搜索面板的列和筛选栏对得更齐。

  **Faster and steadier**: after a quiet while the search box shows at once instead of waiting for a refresh, and new rows no longer arrive a second late now and then while a window is hidden. Switching to English input on the first open after startup is more reliable. With transparency on, the expanded search panel no longer shows an old-style window frame through it, and its columns and filter bar line up better.

- **外观**：外观页的主题卡片只用黑白两色；托盘菜单圆角外不再多一圈方形阴影（截图的菜单也一样）。品牌色彩和触感风格下，选中行的底色不再挡住行里的内容（#443）。序号关掉时，字母直达的预览和按下后的键帽看得出两种状态。欢迎引导的侧边栏换了新背景。

  **Looks**: the theme cards on the Appearance page are plain black and white; the tray menu no longer has a square shadow round its rounded corners (the screenshot's menus neither). In the brand and tactile styles the chosen row's fill no longer covers the row's content (#443). With number keys off, letter jumps show a quiet preview and a clear keycap once armed. The welcome guide's sidebar has a new background.
