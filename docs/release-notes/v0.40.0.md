Win顺 0.40.0 让剪贴板的备注可以选颜色，几类记录一眼分开；设置窗口记住你拖出来的大小，重启后照样，不想要可以在外观里关掉。剪贴板不用搜索的话可以把搜索框藏起来。修好了几处：以管理员身份运行时软件内更新后会回到管理员身份，或者弹通知问你；有道词典划词时复制的文件和图片不再混进剪贴板历史；基于 Everything SDK 的程序按路径搜，找到的就是那个文件夹里的；缩略图没读完就关掉窗口，不再偶尔让程序退出。

- **WinShun-0.40.0-x64-portable.zip** 为便携版。解压运行 `WinShun.exe`，升级时保留原来的 `data` 文件夹。
- **WinShun-0.40.0-x64-offline-setup.exe** 包含增强 OCR。官方可选插件从插件库安装。
- **WinShun-ocr-ppocr-v6-ort1.30.0-x64.zip** 是同版本安装程序使用的增强 OCR 组件。
- **SHA256SUMS.txt** 提供全部附件的 SHA256。

The standard installer keeps settings, the index and clipboard history during an upgrade and downloads enhanced OCR when needed. For the portable edition, keep the existing `data` folder. The offline installer includes enhanced OCR. Optional official plugins are available from the plugin library. Scoop users can run `scoop update winshun` after the bucket update reaches them; with WinGet, `winget upgrade LingCore.WinShun` once the package has caught up.

升级不用迁移任何东西，设置、索引、收藏和剪贴板历史都保留，原来的备注照旧是粗体，加上颜色才变色。截图设置和图片文字搜索的窗口记住大小要“截图与录屏”1.1.28、图片文字搜索 1.0.25，在 设置 › 插件 › 插件库 更新。从 0.36.0 起的版本软件内更新只下载变了的部分。

Nothing needs migrating: settings, the index, favorites and clipboard history are kept, and existing notes stay bold until you give them a colour. For Screenshot settings and Image text search to remember their size, update Screenshot and recording to 1.1.28 and Image text search to 1.0.25 from Settings › Plugins › Plugin library. Updating in the app from 0.36.0 or later downloads only what changed.

## 新功能 · What's new

- 🖍️ **剪贴板备注可以上色**：加备注时，输入框右边有一排颜色（红、橙、黄、绿、蓝、紫、灰），点一下或按 `Tab` / `Shift+Tab` 换，备注就用这个颜色显示，地址、账号、待办这些一眼分得开。颜色和备注一起保存，浅色、深色和各个界面风格下都看得清（#398）。

  **Clipboard notes in colour**: beside the note's box are red, orange, yellow, green, blue, purple and grey; a click or `Tab` / `Shift+Tab` picks one and the note shows in it, so addresses, accounts and to-dos stand apart at a glance. The colour is kept with the note and reads well in light, dark and every style (#398).

- 📐 **设置窗口记住大小**：把设置窗口拖大，下次打开、重启 Win顺 都是这个大小，上次最大化的仍然最大化；仍然开在鼠标所在的屏幕，屏幕放不下就先缩到放得下，回到大屏还是你要的大小。双击窗口边框恢复默认的 920×720。截图设置和图片文字搜索的窗口也一样。不想要就关掉 设置 › 外观 › 记住窗口大小（#401）。

  **The settings window keeps its size**: drag it larger and it opens at that size the next time and after a restart, maximized if you left it so; still on the screen under the pointer, made to fit a smaller screen and back to your size on a larger one. A double click on its edge gives it the default 920 × 720. Screenshot settings and Image text search do the same. Turn off Settings › Appearance › Remember window sizes if you would rather not (#401).

- 🔍 **剪贴板可以不显示搜索框**：不用搜索的话，在 设置 › 剪贴板 关掉“显示搜索框”，窗口顶上只留按钮，打字不再搜索；方向键、`Enter`、`Alt+1`～`9` 和其他快捷键照常，按分组查找时搜索框会临时出现。

  **A clipboard without the search box**: turn off Show the search box under Settings › Clipboard and the top row keeps only its buttons; typing no longer searches, while the arrows, `Enter`, `Alt+1`–`9` and the other shortcuts work as before, and finding a group brings the box back for the name.

## 改进与修复 · Improvements and fixes

- **软件内更新后回到管理员身份**：以管理员身份运行（管理员直读）时，更新完原来要等搜索框、设置、剪贴板全都收起才重启，一直开着就一直是普通权限，也没有任何提示。现在窗口都收起就马上重启；更新完一分钟后还开着，就弹通知“Win顺 已更新”，点一下以管理员身份重启（设置开着的话重启后还开着）。覆盖安装也不再把开机自启改成普通权限（#400）。

  **Back as administrator after an in-app update**: running as administrator (direct disk reading), WinShun waited until the search box, the settings and the clipboard were all put away before restarting, with normal rights and no word for as long as one stayed open. It now restarts as soon as they are put away; a minute after the update with one still open, a notification asks, and a click restarts it as administrator (with the settings open again if they were). Setup over an installed copy no longer turns the startup task to normal rights (#400).

- **其他软件按路径搜也对了**：打开“让其他软件把 Win顺 当作 Everything”后，基于 Everything SDK 的程序发来的 `file:"D:\images" ext:jpg;png`，现在和 Everything 一样找 `D:\images` 里面的 jpg、png，不再是全盘的（#399）。

  **Path searches from other programs**: with WinShun answering for Everything, `file:"D:\images" ext:jpg;png` from a program on the Everything SDK now finds the jpg and png files in `D:\images`, as Everything does, not those on the whole disk (#399).

- **有道词典划词不再往剪贴板历史里塞东西**：它取词时按一下 `Ctrl+C`，前台是文件管理器或微信时复制的是文件、图片，过一会儿再把原来的剪贴板放回去。这种借用一下又放回去的复制，现在不进剪贴板历史；自己复制的照常记录，文字从不受影响。

  **Youdao's selection translation leaves the clipboard history alone**: it presses `Ctrl+C` in the program in front, which copies files or a picture in a file manager or WeChat, and puts the old clipboard back a moment later. Such a borrowed copy no longer lands in the history; your own copies are kept as always, and text is never held back.

- **缩略图不再偶尔让 Win顺 退出**：剪贴板和搜索结果的图片缩略图还没读完，窗口就关了（或者退出 Win顺）时，读完的缩略图会写到已经不在的地方，偶尔让程序直接退出。现在读完没人要就只留着下次用。

  **Thumbnails no longer end WinShun now and then**: when a window was closed (or WinShun quit) while a picture's thumbnail in the clipboard or the results was still being read, the finished thumbnail was written where nothing was left, and the program could quit. Now it is just kept for the next time.
