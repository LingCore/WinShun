Win顺 0.34.2 修了较早的 Windows 10 上的几处问题：托盘图标几乎看不见、深色标题栏不生效、菜单“强制浅色”不起作用、录屏直接失败、截图排除自己窗口时变成一块黑。

## 改进与修复 · Improvements and fixes

- **较早的 Windows 10 上的几处显示问题**：Windows 10 1809 上单色托盘图标画成黑色、几乎看不见，现在按系统的深浅色画；1809 到 1909 上深色标题栏不生效，现在生效；1809 上菜单的“强制浅色”不起作用，现在起作用。

  **Display fixes on older Windows 10**: on 1809 the single-colour tray icon was drawn black and nearly invisible, and now follows the system's light or dark theme; dark title bars now work on 1809 to 1909; forcing light menus now works on 1809.

- **较早的 Windows 10 上的截图和录屏**：1809 到 1909 上录屏一开始就失败，现在能录；Windows 10 上截图直接用系统的老办法截，不再先试一遍新办法再退回来；2004 之前截图时排除 Win顺 自己的窗口会变成一块黑，现在不再这样（截图与录屏 1.1.22）。

  **Screenshots and recording on older Windows 10**: recording failed at once on 1809 to 1909 and now works; on Windows 10 a screenshot goes straight to the older capture method instead of trying the new one first; before 2004, leaving WinShun's own windows out of a screenshot no longer turns them into a black block (Screenshot and recording 1.1.22).

  Screenshot and recording 1.1.22: recording, screenshots and dark title bars on older Windows 10. Permissions unchanged.
